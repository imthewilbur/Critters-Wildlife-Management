Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Call or text (402) 416-6478.

Is the work guaranteed?

All bat work comes with a 2-year warranty. Ask about coverage on other jobs when you call.

A bat got in the house. Now what?

Close interior doors to keep it in one room, keep people and pets out, and call us. If anyone may have been bitten, or woke up with a bat in the room, contact your doctor or the health department about rabies.

Where do you work?
